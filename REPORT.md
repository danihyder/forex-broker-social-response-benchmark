# Forex Brokers Social Response Benchmark: Full Report

This is an independent analysis, not affiliated with, commissioned by, or endorsed by any company
named in it. It reports observable response behaviour on a public review platform. It does not
evaluate the quality, conduct or standing of any broker, and nothing here should be read as an
allegation against one.

## Scope

The study covers four retail forex and CFD brokers: Exness, XM, eToro and Plus500. The sample
comprises 3,466 English-language Trustpilot reviews published between February 2018 and July 2026:
Exness 898, XM 728, eToro 920 and Plus500 920.

### How the sample relates to each broker's full review base

These brokers hold far more reviews than the sample contains. At the time of capture the published
totals were 30,113 for Exness, 3,149 for XM, 31,863 for eToro and 19,571 for Plus500. The sample is
therefore a slice of each review base, not a census of it, for two reasons.

First, Trustpilot's public listing returns roughly 200 reviews per filtered view. Reviews were
collected one star band at a time, giving up to about 200 from each of the five bands per broker,
and a ceiling of roughly 1,000 reviews per broker.

Second, that approach is deliberate rather than merely a constraint. Drawing a similar number from
every star band produces enough 1 and 2 star reviews to analyse complaint handling, which a
proportional sample would not: brokers whose ratings skew positive would otherwise yield very few
complaints. The trade is that the sample does not mirror any broker's true rating distribution, so
every measure in this report is calculated within a rating band and none is presented as a single
blended figure.

Two consequences follow. The sample is weighted towards recent activity, with 81% of sampled
reviews published in 2025 or 2026. And XM's smaller sample reflects its smaller review base, which
does not contain 200 reviews in every band; its 2 star cell holds 68 reviews and its 3 star cell
100, so percentages for those bands rest on fewer observations than the other brokers'.

Reply-type and template classifications are keyword and similarity heuristics. Coverage is limited
to the Trustpilot `.com` view and therefore to English-language reviews.

## Response rate

Exness replied to between 78% and 93% of its 1-3 star reviews and to very few of its positive
reviews. XM, eToro and Plus500 replied across all five bands, including 88-100% of positive reviews.

| Response rate by rating | Exness | XM | eToro | Plus500 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1★ | 77.6% | 79.5% | 100% | 99.4% |
| 2★ | 93.0% | 91.2% | 91.2% | 85.6% |
| 3★ | 87.8% | 92.0% | 85.6% | 98.9% |
| 4★ | 1.1% | 100% | 100% | 97.8% |
| 5★ | 0% | 100% | 100% | 91.2% |

This represents two distinct engagement models: one concentrating response effort on negative
feedback, the other maintaining a public presence across every rating.

## Response speed

Median time to a first public reply was 14.1 hours at eToro, 17.4 at Plus500, 24.8 at XM and 43.4 at
Exness, a spread of roughly threefold between fastest and slowest.

## Reply type

Replies were classified into three types. A substantive reply addresses the issue in the public
thread; a redirect moves the customer to a private channel; a regulator referral defers to an
external dispute body.

| Share of replies | Exness | XM | eToro | Plus500 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Substantive | 47.9% | 71.0% | 79.0% | 67.8% |
| Redirect to private channel | 51.0% | 23.0% | 6.5% | 25.3% |
| Regulator or ombudsman referral | 1.1% | 5.9% | 14.5% | 6.9% |

Redirects frequently reflect regulatory constraint rather than avoidance, as a regulated broker
generally cannot discuss an individual account or an open dispute publicly. Reply type is therefore
reported separately from reply rate.

## Review provenance

The share of positive reviews (4-5 star) arriving organically rather than through a company
invitation was 26.4% at eToro, 9.5% at Exness, 7.8% at XM and 1.6% at Plus500. Complaints arrived
organically for every broker. Published star ratings consequently reflect review-solicitation
practice in addition to customer sentiment.

## Complaint topics

The following shows the share of each broker's 1-2 star reviews mentioning each topic. A review may
carry more than one topic.

- **Exness:** withdrawals 27%, deposits 24%, platform and execution 22%, scam or fraud claims 15%,
  verification 14%.
- **XM:** deposits 46%, withdrawals 37%, scam or fraud claims 30%, support 24%, payment rails 23%,
  verification 19%.
- **eToro:** support responsiveness 30%, withdrawals 21%, deposits 16%, payment rails 16%,
  verification 15%, scam or fraud claims 15%.
- **Plus500:** withdrawals 20%, deposits 9%, scam or fraud claims 9%, support 8%, platform and
  execution 7%.

Money movement, covering withdrawals and deposits, led the complaint mix for every broker.

## Complaint geography

Countries contributing the most complaints, by broker:

- **Exness:** India, Pakistan, UAE, South Africa, Bangladesh, Nigeria.
- **XM:** India, Pakistan, South Africa, UAE, Malaysia, Turkey.
- **eToro:** United Kingdom, Denmark, Greece, Australia, Netherlands, Ireland.
- **Plus500:** United Kingdom, Australia, United States, UAE, South Africa, Netherlands.

## Distinctive complaint language

Ranking complaint phrases by raw frequency returns the same generic terms for every broker, such as
"customer service" and "trading account". Ranking instead by distinctiveness, meaning how much more
frequently a phrase occurs for one broker than for the other three combined, isolates the language
specific to each. Phrases below occur in at least five reviews.

These are the words reviewers themselves used. They show what customers complained about and how
they described it. They are not verified claims about any broker's conduct.

- **eToro:** cash isa (34 reviews, 143x), isa account (9), open cash (7), non existent (6), hidden
  fees (6). Reviewers referring to the UK cash ISA product and to account opening.
- **XM:** deposited inr (10 reviews, 65x), deposit inr (8), credited trading (8), successfully
  debited (7), deposit scam (7), bank wire (6). Reviewers describing rupee deposits taken from their
  bank account but, in their account, not appearing in the trading account.
- **Exness:** million usd (12 reviews, 75x), margin levels (6), manipulates margin (6), financial
  commission (5), deposited million (5). Reviewers describing margin and stop-out disputes, and
  referring to escalation to the Financial Commission.
- **Plus500:** high spread (10 reviews), spread high (9), open positions (9), send money (6).
  Reviewers referring to spread pricing and position handling.

## Review length

Average review length declined with each additional star: 112 words at 1 star, 75 at 2 stars, 48 at
3 stars, 28 at 4 stars and 21 at 5 stars. Dissatisfied customers supplied substantially more detail
than satisfied ones.
